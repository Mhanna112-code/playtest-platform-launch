# Owner decisions, November 7, 2026

These decisions replace the earlier rules that kept departed testers' keys attached for rejoining.

## 1. Keys

When a tester leaves, their work ends early, or their account is deleted, unassign their access key and return it to Free. Remove the assignment from In use; do not keep a grey row. Retain the assigned key and its assignment date in their Terms history. Rejoining uses the normal free-key assignment; their former key can be reused if it is still free. Preserve older assignments before clearing them. No automatic spot reduction is requested.

## 2. Terms history

Keep the existing question-change history. Add dated join, leave, end-early and rejoin events and key assignments. The developer's history also shows parts/price offers and dated Keep/Switch decisions; the tester's existing offer history remains. Do not add NDA acceptance to Terms history, and remove its badge from the developer's tester heading. Keep the NDA acknowledgement rules.

## 3. Tester selection

If the selected tester is no longer working on the test, or their account was deleted, return the Testers filter to All testers. Retain their historical work.

## 4. Terms of Use

Explain that saved edits update the current listing for existing testers where relevant. Keep/Switch protects their agreed parts and prices; it does not preserve older questions or other listing information. NDA changes are acknowledged separately. This is a clarification of the existing behavior before launch, folded into Terms version 2.

## 5. Paused header and tester earnings

A developer's paused test header keeps its listing tags, including the ordinary status and free-key count, and adds Paused. The controls still obey the pause restrictions. A tester's earnings must not name the developer's Pay what you owe repayment method; the developer's own payment details may still explain it. Everything you sent and Your earnings retain their separate work-status and payment-history purposes.

## 6. Hidden Required flags on Edit

Turning a part on and off while editing its questions must not fabricate a save in another tab. Retain the stored Required checkbox value when its part is off, including in the comparison baseline. Genuine changes saved in another tab still use the normal conflict notice.

## 7. Withdrawn applications

An application withdrawn by a tester adds a quiet live line for the developer, alongside the existing bell notice and email setting. It never adds a stop-once reason. Accept or Decline on the withdrawn application remains a final refusal. This replaces the earlier bell-only rule in batch 35 section 185.
