# Why double pay isn't possible

Every path that takes a developer's money, what could charge twice on it, the guard in the code, and the proof (an automated test with a fake Stripe that remembers
each PaymentIntent and refuses to cancel one that already went through, and/or a recording in this folder made against Stripe TEST mode).

Three guards repeat everywhere:

- **The one-step claim.** A row changes state in one conditional update (`updateMany … where status/actionPi = what I saw`); only the first of two racing requests
  gets `count === 1` and goes on, the other stops.
- **Cancel at Stripe first.** Whenever the app stops tracking a bank check it cancels the PaymentIntent at Stripe before letting go (`owedOnce.dropCheck`). If Stripe
  says it had already gone through, it's recorded as paid instead (nothing is charged again).
- **Idempotency keys.** Every Stripe call that creates a charge carries a key derived from the press or the row (`payKey`, `serverKey("owedall", id)`,
  `serverKey("deferred", id)`, `capture-<reservation>`), so a retried request gets Stripe's first answer, never a second charge.

**The only way a second payment follows Pay what you owe** (owner, §125): a charge that comes in while Pay what you owe's one payment is in flight (being charged,
or its bank check open: 15 minutes for a press, 3 days for a retry) is not added to it (its amount is fixed at the claim). It waits ("Charged right after the payment
above goes through") and is made right after the lift as one combined payment. Test: `batch35-payment-proofs` "a charge arriving while the one payment is in flight";
clip `G-full-chain.webm`.
