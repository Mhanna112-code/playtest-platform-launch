# Social launch copy — revised November 7

These replace the earlier launch drafts. Publish them yourself when the site is ready; nothing has been posted. The refreshed reels are caption-led promotional graphics, not recordings of the current interface. They are silent, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps and 16 seconds each. Preview in the destination app before publishing because its controls can cover different parts of the frame.

Revised exports are in `launch-reels/revised/`. The original reels remain in the owner's `launch-reels/` folder. Rebuild with `node scripts/build-social-reels.cjs` (set `FFMPEG_PATH` if needed).

## What changed from Claude's drafts

- Removed “in seconds,” “matching testers join instantly” as a marketing promise, “Booked in one tap,” and the absolute “Never lose your work.”
- Use “80% of each payment,” not “80% of every price.” Developers may pay below the listed price with a reason, and testers can dispute within their window.
- Charge applies to on-time, listed written work and recordings still unpaid. It opens at the later of sent + seven days and the tester's deadline. Listed calls use automatic charging; do not advertise the written-work choice as universal.
- One dispute is per piece, not per payment. A Pay more does not create another dispute allowance. Rating/close-out can end the ability to open it.
- The earlier NDA/key reel's “This key doesn't work” beat is obsolete: §304 removed that button. Tell the developer in chat. NDA changes now need acknowledgement.
- The earlier “your deal stays yours” script needs narrower wording: agreed parts and prices use Switch/Keep; questions and listing information follow the current listing (§265). Do not imply testers can keep an obsolete question set.
- Screens from October 31 are useful source material, but do not present them as current UI proof: field limits, parts labels and other details have changed. Preserve them separately.
- No promises about how quickly testers arrive, guaranteed earnings, guaranteed payment amounts or permanent draft backups. No assumed launch date, publishing time or unverified community permission.
