# Batch 34 (owner, Oct 30)

This file wins over the earlier batches and the design doc where they differ.

## 1. Admins: opposite actions stop once; impossible ones name who and when (Claude)
- Every admin press sends its page time. When another admin (or the app) changed the SAME account, test, hold, dispute or outage since then and the press is still possible, it stops once in a blue box: "Your Delete didn't go through. Since this page loaded: admin2@… reinstated the tester side at 12:19 AM. Press Delete again to go ahead." The second press goes ahead.
  Pairs: Delete after a Suspend / Reinstate / $0 close-out; the $0 close-out after a Suspend / Reinstate; Pause after Lift the pause or Stop; Lift the pause after a Pause or Close now; Stop after Lift the pause / Extend; Close now ↔ Extend; chargeback Approve ↔ Don't approve; Approve a payment-hold lift after a Suspend; outage Approve ↔ Dismiss; Allow changes again; Mark replied.
- A press that's no longer possible is a plain refusal naming who and when: "That test was already paused (by admin2@… at 12:19 AM).", "…already stopped (by … at …)", "That hold was already approved (by … at …)", "That account was already deleted (by … at …)".
- Tests for every pair; screenshots of two admins side by side for each kind (desktop).

## 2. Accept / Decline, Invite and Cancel invite: stop only for what matters, named (Codex task 5)
- Accept and Decline on an application (and an ask to rejoin) stop once only when, since the page loaded, the tester's profile changed in a way that matters to THIS test (a device or language the test needs added or removed), named exactly as Invite names it ("fbbb no longer lists Chinese, which this test needs."), or their rating changed by 1 star or more ("fbbb's rating went from 4.6 to 3.5"). Any other profile save (bio, other devices, categories, a save with no change) no longer stops them, and the vague "changed their profile" line goes.
- Invite and Cancel invite stop the same way for those two reasons (Invite already does for the needs; the rating change is new; Cancel invite gets both).
- One line per tester (a newer change replaces their line), one time each.

## 3. The reserved Pay what you owe (Codex task 6)
- With a reservation in place the button (still switched off, its style unchanged: owner, Oct 30, no grey) reads "$10.00 is charged after the team approves" instead of "Pay what you owe ($10.00)".
- (Owner, Oct 30) The developer's line no longer adds "$10.00 added since will be charged right after.": it reads only "Reserved $27.00: it's taken when the team approves lifting your hold." (the greyed button says the rest). The admin's chargeback row keeps its "added since (charged after the hold lifts)".

## 5. The two sides look like two separate accounts; only the switch button changes sides (owner, Oct 30) (Codex task 8)
- **Only the switch button changes sides** (the header's ⇆ and the Account page's "Switch to your … side"). Nothing else does: not opening a page of the other side (a link, a bell line, an email link, a typed address, another tab), not coming back from Stripe, not the side-switch link page. The app's layout no longer remembers a page's side as the side used last (src/app/app/layout.tsx), and batch 32 §16 (instant /app/switch) and §17 (Stripe returns set the side) are undone: the Stripe returns leave the side as it is. **The in-between "Switch to your … side and continue" page goes** (owner, Oct 30): links to the other side's pages (sideLink, bell lines, emails) go straight to the page, which shows the notice below; /app/switch's GET just redirects to the page asked for (old links in emails keep working) without switching, and only its POST (the switch button) changes the side.
- **A page of the other side** (e.g. /app/find, /app/sessions/…, /app/developers/… while on the developer side; /app/tests/…, /app/testers/… while on the tester side) shows only a notice instead of the page: "This page is on your tester side." with the button "Switch to your tester side" (a POST to /app/switch that then opens that page). The header and everything around it stay on the current side.
- **Notifications belong to a side:** every bell line and email line is the developer's, the tester's, or the account's (sign-in, password, Terms, account deletion, suspension of the whole account). The bell, its number and the Notifications page show only the current side's lines plus the account's. Existing rows are sorted by their link and kind (a migration with a backfill; nothing is lost). The emails keep one email per account, with the lines under "As a developer" / "As a tester" headings.
- **Deleting the account says it's both sides** (owner, Oct 30): for an account with both sides, the delete warning starts "This deletes your whole account: your developer side and your tester side, on both sides at once." and the Delete button's confirm repeats it; on either side's Account page the same.
- **Shared pages are per side:** Payments, Account and Notifications show only the current side's content. The delete-account warning mentions "anything you owe (a payment hold, a chargeback)" only when the account has a developer side that owes something; the notification settings' help lists only the current side's kinds (developer: receipts, failures, holds, retries, chargebacks; tester: payments, delays, payouts).
- **Proof (owner):** the report shows nothing leaking: for an account with both sides and money history on both, every page of each side, the bell list and an email, searched for the other side's words.
