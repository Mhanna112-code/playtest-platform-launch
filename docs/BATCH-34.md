# Batch 34 (owner, Oct 30)

This file wins over the earlier batches and the design doc where they differ.

## 1. Admins: opposite actions stop once; impossible ones name who and when (Claude)
- Every admin press sends its page time. When another admin (or the app) changed the SAME account, test, hold, dispute or outage since then and the press is still possible, it stops once in a blue box: "Your Delete didn't go through. Since this page loaded: admin2@… reinstated the tester side at 12:19 AM. Press Delete again to go ahead." The second press goes ahead.
  Pairs: Delete after a Suspend / Reinstate / $0 close-out; the $0 close-out after a Suspend / Reinstate; Pause after Lift the pause or Stop; Lift the pause after a Pause or Close now; Stop after Lift the pause / Extend; Close now ↔ Extend; chargeback Approve ↔ Don't approve; Approve a payment-hold lift after a Suspend; outage Approve ↔ Dismiss; Allow changes again; Mark replied.
- A press that's no longer possible is a plain refusal naming who and when: "That test was already paused (by admin2@… at 12:19 AM).", "…already stopped (by … at …)", "That hold was already approved (by … at …)", "That account was already deleted (by … at …)".
- Tests for every pair; screenshots of two admins side by side for each kind (desktop).

## 2. Accept / Decline, Invite and Cancel invite: stop only for what matters, named (Codex task 5)
- Accept and Decline on an application (and an ask to rejoin) stop once only when, since the page loaded, the tester's profile changed in a way that matters to THIS test (a device or language the test needs added or removed), named exactly as Invite names it ("fbbb no longer lists Chinese, which this test needs."), or their rating changed by 1 star or more ("fbbb's rating went from 4.6 to 3.5"). Any other profile save (bio, other devices, categories, a save with no change) no longer stops them, and the vague "changed their profile" line goes.
- Invite and Cancel invite stop the same way for those two reasons (Invite already does for the needs; the rating change is new; Cancel invite gets both).
- One line per tester (a newer change replaces their line), one time each.

## 3. The reserved Pay what you owe (Codex task 6)
- With a reservation in place the button (still switched off, its style unchanged: owner, Oct 30, no grey) reads "$10.00 is charged after the team approves" instead of "Pay what you owe ($10.00)".
- (Owner, Oct 30) The developer's line no longer adds "$10.00 added since will be charged right after.": it reads only "Reserved $27.00: it's taken when the team approves lifting your hold." (the greyed button says the rest). The admin's chargeback row keeps its "added since (charged after the hold lifts)".

## 4. No Reinstate of the developer side during a hold (owner, Oct 30)
- An admin can't reinstate a developer's DEVELOPER side (lift the suspension) while that developer is on a payment hold or a chargeback hold. On the profile's admin box (and the console's Suspensions) the developer side's Reinstate is replaced by a note: "Reinstate waits for the payment hold: they pay what they owe first." / "Reinstate waits for the chargeback hold: the team approves it first."
- The server refuses an old page's press the same way: "That developer is on a payment hold: it has to clear before the developer side can be reinstated." (or "chargeback hold").
- The tester side's Reinstate is unaffected. The live redraw brings Reinstate back when the hold clears.
- Tests for both holds and the tester side; a screenshot in the admin run.

## 5. The two sides look like two separate accounts; only the switch button changes sides (owner, Oct 30) (Codex task 8)
- **Only the switch button changes sides** (the header's ⇆ and the Account page's "Switch to your … side"). Nothing else does: not opening a page of the other side (a link, a bell line, an email link, a typed address, another tab), not coming back from Stripe, not the side-switch link page. The app's layout no longer remembers a page's side as the side used last (src/app/app/layout.tsx), and batch 32 §16 (instant /app/switch) and §17 (Stripe returns set the side) are undone: the Stripe returns leave the side as it is. **The in-between "Switch to your … side and continue" page goes** (owner, Oct 30): links to the other side's pages (sideLink, bell lines, emails) go straight to the page, which shows the notice below; /app/switch's GET just redirects to the page asked for (old links in emails keep working) without switching, and only its POST (the switch button) changes the side.
- **A page of the other side** (e.g. /app/find, /app/sessions/…, /app/developers/… while on the developer side; /app/tests/…, /app/testers/… while on the tester side) shows only a notice instead of the page: "This page is on your tester side." with the button "Switch to your tester side" (a POST to /app/switch that then opens that page). The header and everything around it stay on the current side.
- **Notifications belong to a side:** every bell line and email line is the developer's, the tester's, or the account's (sign-in, password, Terms, account deletion, suspension of the whole account). The bell, its number and the Notifications page show only the current side's lines plus the account's. Existing rows are sorted by their link and kind (a migration with a backfill; nothing is lost). The emails keep one email per account, with the lines under "As a developer" / "As a tester" headings.
- **Deleting the account says it's both sides** (owner, Oct 30): for an account with both sides, the delete warning starts "This deletes your whole account: both your developer side and your tester side." and the Delete button's confirm repeats it; on either side's Account page the same.
- **Shared pages are per side:** Payments, Account and Notifications show only the current side's content. The delete-account warning mentions "anything you owe (a payment hold, a chargeback)" only when the account has a developer side that owes something; the notification settings' help lists only the current side's kinds (developer: receipts, failures, holds, retries, chargebacks; tester: payments, delays, payouts).
- **Proof (owner):** the report shows nothing leaking: for an account with both sides and money history on both, every page of each side, the bell list and an email, searched for the other side's words.

## 6. A card before posting; expiry warnings; "Get started" (owner, Oct 30) (Codex task 9)
- **Every test needs a saved card to post**, priced or not (a draft can be saved without one). Post the test without a card is refused: "Add a card in Payments before posting: testers' pay is charged to it." with a link to Payments; the Post a test page says it up front near Post ("Posting needs a card in Payments.", with Add a card) while there's none. A card is only saved after Stripe's card page confirms it with the bank, so a dead card can't be saved; there's no way to remove a card, only to change it.
- **Expiry:** Stripe gives each saved card's expiry month and year (keep them when the card is saved and refresh them from Stripe on the daily job, since Stripe often updates an expiring card by itself). A month before the card expires, a week before, and on the day: the developer's bell line and email "Your card ending 4242 expires at the end of March: update it in Payments." (account-level money line, emails even with email Off, like other payment lines). If it isn't updated and a charge fails, the existing path applies (payment hold, Pay what you owe, add a new card).
- **A card that's gone at Stripe is a decline** (owner, Oct 30: keep it simple before launch, nothing until a charge fails): a charge whose card or customer Stripe no longer knows ("No such customer" / "No such payment_method" / resource_missing) goes the same way as a declined card: the payment hold, Pay what you owe after adding a new card, and the tester reads only "Payment for your work may be delayed". No daily check of saved cards, no separate card hold, and an expired card is only acted on when a charge fails (the warnings above still go out).
- **"Get started"** on the dashboard, right under the person's name, for a new account, staying until every step is done: developer: "Add a card (needed to post a test)"; tester: "Set up payouts (to receive your pay)". Each step links to Payments and shows ✓ once done; the box goes when all are done. An account with both sides sees each side's steps on that side.

## 7. A developer's open tests and drafts have different names (owner, Oct 30)
- Post the test (or posting a draft) with the name of one of the developer's open tests or drafts (same letters, any case, spaces trimmed) is refused: "You already have a test called “Harbor Run” (posted Fri, Oct 30, 11:00 AM): give this one a different name." with a link to that test; a Save renaming a test into such a name is refused the same way. Other developers' tests and stopped tests don't count. Two Posts at the same moment (two tabs) take turns, so only one gets the name.

## 8. A save in another tab is one line naming its fields (owner's finding, Oct 30)
- The developer's own save in another tab reads "You saved the listing in another tab: title (now “X”), description; this re-sent fbbb's invite" (it used to say only "You changed the listing in another tab: re-sent fbbb's invite", with the rename on its own line). A rename alone stays its own info-only line.

## 9. Edit after its own Save (owner's finding, Oct 30)
- Repro: fields changed on Edit, Save went through (11:15); another tab, then back (maybe Back), Save again: it stopped with "Product / Name of this test / Instructions: your version will replace the one saved in another tab at 11:15 AM." although that save was theirs from this same page, with no blue "Saved in another tab or device at …" note under those fields; and right after a Save a change-limit line ("Changes left…" / "can't be saved: you've changed … 3 times") flashed.
- After a Save goes through, the page's base (the version and every field's saved value, the free keys) becomes what was just saved and the kept draft ("Not saved yet") is cleared (by the page and by the server), so a tab switch, a Back / forward return (bfcache: `pageshow` with `persisted` refreshes the data) or a draft restore never brings older text back.
- A save made from this same browser tab is never "another tab": when every listing save since the page loaded was this tab's own (each save keeps its tab), Save has nothing to stop for. A field the tab already holds the saved value of (a live refresh) no longer counts as news to it either.
- Whenever Save stops with "<field>: your version will replace the one saved in another tab at …", that field shows its blue "Saved in another tab or device at …" note with the saved value and Use theirs (also after a fresh load with a restored draft).
- No flash of the change-limit line after a successful Save: the page stays as saved ("Saving…") until the test page shows.

## 10. Join after a re-sent invite (owner's finding, Oct 30)

- If the refusal still happens (the change was already shown), it's one sentence, no dangling colon: "Nora Lane changed the listing at 11:16 AM and sent you a new invite on it. Press Join to join on it."; the page switches to the new invite and the next press joins.

## 14. The developer's test page shows the current listing; listing lines link to it (owner, Oct 30) (Codex task 10)

A field changed recently carries an "Updated <time>" tag opening Before / Now (each in a scroll box of about 8 lines), like the tester's "Your agreed scope".
- Every blue box on the test page (the top box and the stop boxes under any button) shows a listing change as ONE short line with a green **See it** link that jumps to the Listing panel ("You saved the listing in another tab at 11:16 AM: description, build link · See it"); the details live in the panel.
- Edit stays as it is ("You saved the listing in another tab…" in the stop box, the blue "Saved in another tab or device at …" note with Use theirs under each changed field).
- (Owner, Oct 30, refinement) The panel REPLACES the "Contract changes" fold (its save history of before → after per field). Only the fields changed in the MOST RECENT save carry the "Updated <time>" tag (Before / Now); an older save's fields have none. The developer's own Stop reason, which that fold showed, reads in the red "This test is stopped." banner (each tester's row keeps theirs). (Built by Claude, Oct 30.)

They never stop Pay, Pay more, End early, call presses, Edit, Next round, Stop the test, or any of the tester's own presses.
